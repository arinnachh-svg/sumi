Silence before the line

Hana · 2025 · ink, washi paper

Description

A single brushstroke on rice paper. A work about the pause before action, when intent has not yet become a line. The emptiness around the stroke carries as much meaning as the stroke itself.

Alt-description for screen reader

A horizontal black ink stroke in the centre of a light sheet. Below it a short grey stroke. To the right, a round red seal.
